Clear scope before access

Essential Insights is limited to approved controlled-beta participants and eligible datasets. ProgramMetrics does not treat public pages as paid analysis, assume that every input is compatible, or promise open-ended product use.

Where does Studio run?

Executable Studio functionality belongs in the private application. The public website explains the offer and launch status.

How are files handled?

Studio is designed for browser-local processing of properly de-identified, structured data by default. Browser-local processing does not eliminate privacy risk, and customers remain responsible for de-identification.

Are the offers bundled?

No bundle is implied. A one-time order, Studio subscription, or template license includes only what its terms state.

Apply for the limited private beta using only fictional or properly de-identified data. Applying does not activate an account, authorize a dataset, or create a purchase.
