Controlled-beta access

Essential Insights is being validated in the private Studio.

Selected controlled-beta participants can use verified account and entitlement access for browser-local analysis of one eligible, properly de-identified CSV. General customer access and recurring Studio subscriptions are not yet available.

Review Planned Studio Review Data Rules

Before requesting beta access

Review the product boundary and data restrictions. Do not submit or upload a dataset through the public website or application form.

Customer responsibility

Customers must de-identify data before any future submission and confirm their legal authority to use it. ProgramMetrics does not perform legal de-identification or certify HIPAA or FERPA compliance.

No identifiers or free text

Identifying fields, regulated identifiable data, case notes, comments, and other open-ended free text will be prohibited at initial launch.

Anonymous identifiers only

Anonymous record identifiers may be used only when ProgramMetrics cannot identify or contact an individual. Re-identification keys must remain outside ProgramMetrics.
